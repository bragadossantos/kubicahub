# FKCU Domain & Governance Orchestrator

## 1. The Multidisciplinary Synergy Matrix (MSM)
Every venture incubated in Kubica Hub must allocate 4 distinct C-Level roles:
- **Engineering & Tech (`CTO`):** MVP code, software architecture, technical documentation.
- **Business & Economics (`CEO / CFO`):** Canvas, financial modeling, unit economics, operations.
- **Law (`CLO`):** MoU, Co-founders Agreement, 3-year Vesting, IAPI trademark/patent filings (Lei n.º 3/92).
- **Marketing & Languages (`CMO`):** Visual identity, copywriting, growth channels, English pitch deck.

## 2. 4-Phase Lifecycle Rules
- **Phase 1: Intake & Triage (`IDE-01` to `IDE-04`):** Student inventors submit ideas; builders register skills. Hub evaluates feasibility and alignment with Angola's PDN 2023–2027.
- **Phase 2: Matchmaking (`MAT-01` to `MAT-05`):** 60-second Speed Dating + Vanderbilt alignment check. Ends with a signed preliminary MoU.
- **Phase 3: Co-Creation & Incubation (`INC-01` to `INC-06`):** Dual mentorship (Faculty Tutor + Industry Mentor), Stanford d.school Design Thinking sprints, MVP building.
- **Phase 4: Graduation (`GRA-01` to `GRA-04`):** INAPEM Startup Label certification (Lei das Startups 2026), IAPI priority filing, and University Demo Day pitch.

## 3. Governance & Legal Parameters
- **IP Sharing (USIT Guide Standard):** 100% Student Equity if independent IP; 90% Team / 10% University if utilizing university lab patents.
- **Vesting Standard:** 3-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff.
- **Sandbox Grants (MIT Model):** Up to USD 25,000 non-dilutive funding disbursed against milestones, reimbursement-only via verified invoices.
