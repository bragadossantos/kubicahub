# Kubica Hub Brand Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Philosophy
Kubica Hub represents the forging of Angola's new technological industries inside universities. The aesthetic is warm, high-contrast, editorial, and tactile — combining academic authority with the drive of an innovation studio.

## 1. Color Palette

### Base Canvas & Surfaces
- **Espresso (`#140b00`):** Dominant dark canvas for all pages and panels.
- **Midnight Cocoa (`#0b0600`):** Recessed dark surface for footers, funding dials, and secondary containers.
- **Soft Cream (`#f1f1f1`):** Inverted light surface reserved for legal agreements and IAPI documents.

### Typography & Text Ink
- **Warm Cream (`#fff1e0`):** Primary text, headlines, and icon strokes. Reads like warm editorial parchment.
- **Driftwood (`#85796c`):** Secondary metadata, helper text, and descriptions.

### Borders & Structural Lines
- **Walnut (`#43392d`):** 1px hairline borders for cards, dividers, and faculty module boundaries.
- **Cedar (`#4f4538`):** Input field borders and neutral checkbox states.
- **Burnt Edge (`#181109`):** Subtle internal depth lines.

### Chromatic Signal Action
- **Amber Forge (`#ffb442`):** THE ONLY chromatic accent. Used for primary CTAs, co-founder connection triggers, active badges, and focus rings.

---

## 2. Typography Hierarchy: PP Neue Montreal

The system uses **PP Neue Montreal** exclusively across all UI elements (Substitutes: `Inter`, `Manrope`).
- **Weights Allowed:** `400 (Regular)` for body/data; `500 (Medium)` for headlines and emphasis. NO weights 600+ or generic bolding.
- **Display Scale:**
  - `Hero XL`: 81px | Line Height: 0.80 | Letter Spacing: `-1.13px`
  - `Hero`: 73px | Line Height: 1.00 | Letter Spacing: `-0.80px`
  - `Display`: 43px | Line Height: 1.10 | Letter Spacing: `-0.30px`
  - `Heading SM`: 22px | Line Height: 1.20 | Letter Spacing: `-0.05px`
  - `Body`: 14px–16px | Line Height: 1.50 | Letter Spacing: `normal`
  - `Caption / Tags`: 11px–12px | Line Height: 1.40 | Letter Spacing: `0.10em` (ALL CAPS)

---

## 3. Component Design Rules
1. **Buttons & Inputs:** ALWAYS use full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`).
2. **Cards & Containers:** ALWAYS use structured soft corners (`border-radius: 6px`).
3. **No Blur Shadows:** Never use diffuse drop-shadows or glows. Elevation is achieved purely through 1px Walnut borders and color contrast against Espresso.
